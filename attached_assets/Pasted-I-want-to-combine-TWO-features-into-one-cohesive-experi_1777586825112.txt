- Max 3 lines
- Bold the amount and commitment name
- Always end with emoji
- Never formal MSA, always conversational

- 🟡 due within 5 days
- 🟢 due within 7 days
- Clicking a row → close panel + scroll to that commitment

- Click outside or [✕] → slides back out
- position: fixed, z-index: 100
- Dark backdrop overlay behind it
- Stateless — always reflects current live data
