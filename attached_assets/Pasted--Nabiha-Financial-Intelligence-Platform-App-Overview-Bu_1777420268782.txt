# نَبِيهَة (Nabiha) — Financial Intelligence Platform

## App Overview

Build a web application called **نبيهة (Nabiha)** — a financial intelligence platform designed to help junior employees manage their first salary with confidence.

**Primary language:** Arabic (RTL layout) **Exception:** Keep brand/product names in English (e.g. Netflix, Adobe, Gmail)

---

## Core Features

### 1. Authentication & Profile

- Users can sign up and log in (email/password and Gmail OAuth)
- After signing in, users set their monthly salary and preferred currency (default: JOD; support all major Arab region currencies)
- Salary should be editable at any time from the dashboard

---

### 2. Subscription Tracker

- Users can add, edit, and delete recurring subscriptions (e.g. streaming services, SaaS tools)
- Each subscription has a name, amount, renewal frequency, and active/inactive status
- The dashboard shows a summary: total active subscriptions and how much of the salary they consume
- Visualize the breakdown of subscriptions as a chart (e.g. donut chart) showing each subscription's share of the total salary

---

### 3. Financial Calendar

- A calendar view showing the user's financial commitments and personal events throughout the year
- Users can add events: subscription renewals, personal occasions, holidays, and custom financial events
- Clicking a month shows a detailed timeline of all events and payments due that month
- Events can be one-time or recurring

---

### 4. Borrowing Capacity Simulator

- A tool that lets users simulate the impact of taking a loan on their monthly budget
- Inputs: loan amount, interest rate, repayment period
- Output: estimated monthly payment and a clear signal (safe / risky / not recommended) based on remaining salary after all commitments
- Show a projected balance chart over the loan period

---

### 5. Notifications & Alerts

- Notify users before upcoming financial commitments (e.g. 3 days before, 1 day before, day of)
- Alert when projected balance drops below a safe threshold
- Notifications appear in-app and optionally via email
- Email alerts should be RTL-friendly, clearly formatted, and include actionable suggestions

---

## Design Direction

- Clean, modern fintech aesthetic with an approachable, friendly tone
- RTL layout throughout
- Color scheme: use purple/blue as primary, with green for positive states, orange/red for warnings
- Fully responsive: mobile, tablet, and desktop

---

## Brand Voice

Nabiha is a helpful financial companion — supportive, clear, and empowering. The tagline is: **"درهمك بأمان مع نبيهة الزمان"**
